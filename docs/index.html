Your film folders, finally a library.

Kino turns the movies on your PC into a catalog with posters and ratings.

Download for Windows Get the Android app

It knows what you have.

Kino reads the file names, strips the release tags and finds each film: poster, year, genres and a description in your language. A wrong guess takes one click to fix.

Four ratings, and one that leads.

IMDb
Leads every card. Filled in from the official IMDb dataset, with no key to set up.
Kinopoisk
Titles, posters and descriptions in Russian.
TMDB
One more source, switched on in settings.
Letterboxd
The film’s rating, and your watchlist imported from your profile.

Start on the PC. Finish on the sofa.

  1. Turn on TV access in Kino’s settings.
  2. Install kino.apk on an Android TV or phone.
  3. Pick your PC, choose the audio and subtitles, and carry on where you stopped.

Small things that keep it tidy.

Watchlist
Add films by title or pull the whole list from Letterboxd.
Pin, hide, watched
Right-click a card to pin it on top, hide it or move it to Watched.
Another PC
Open the library of a second computer on your network.
Stays current
Kino checks GitHub for a new version when it starts.

Free.

Kino is open source. Your files never leave your computer.
